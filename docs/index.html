Who We Are

Powering Sustainable Development Through Renewable Energy

Afri Renewable Energy Company Limited was established with a vision of making renewable technologies a reliable, affordable, and convenient source of power and clean water for both rural and urban communities.

AREN combines technical expertise, community engagement, and innovative technologies to address some of Malawi’s most important challenges, including energy poverty, unsafe water, climate impacts, agricultural vulnerability, and dependence on harmful energy sources.

Our Vision

To make solar energy the reliable, affordable, and convenient source of power for both rural and urban areas in Malawi.

Our Mission

To provide cost-effective renewable energy and clean water solutions for domestic and commercial use.
